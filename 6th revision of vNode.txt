MC2-01 | Fixed `MIN_SIGNATURES` in `executeSetTransaction` Can Lead to a Deadlock Situation Where No Transactions can be Executed
Medium
Recommendation
If the team is still using the current contract or similar design in other contracts, we recommend paying close attention to the number of _validOwners and ensure it never falls below three. To fully resolve this issue, we recommend refining the mechanism for adjusting minimum signatures required.

Modified：
1. add require in the RemoveOwner() function for validowners count should be more than 3;

uint ownerCount =  validOwners.length;
require(ownerCount - 1 >= MIN_SIGNATURES,"valid Owners can not less then 3! ");

2. change the require check the signatureCount should more than or equal to the validowner count;

uint ownerCount =  validOwners.length;
uint signCount = ownerCount>=3?MIN_SIGNATURES:ownerCount;
require(transaction.signatureCount >= signCount,"Signs Not Satisfied");
